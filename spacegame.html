Spider Horror Game

One thing is said about this game... CHAOS. From aliens to asteroids this game is crazy. Dodger alien bullets and asteroids! How long can you survive?

🎮 Controls

  • W or Up arrow to move forward
  • A and D or arrows to rotate left and right
  • R to respawn
  • Escape to pause
  • Mobile Controls are in beta
